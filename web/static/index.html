      Keyboard & mouse
      
    

    

      
Click
cut a word / restore it

      
Drag
cut a range of words

      
⌥ Click
restore, never cut

      
Right-click
correct the word's text (↵ save, esc cancel)

      
Double-click
move the playhead there

      
Space
play / pause the preview

      
⌘Z
undo

      
⌘/
this list
